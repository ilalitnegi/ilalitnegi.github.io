Research Interests

FIELD NOTES

My primary research interests lie at the intersection of Geographic Information Systems (GIS), simulation, Machine Learning (ML) and Augmented/Virtual Reality (AR/VR). I am particularly interested in geospatial modeling and simulation, 3D GIS, immersive geospatial visualization, spatial analysis, and the integration of GIS with AR/VR technologies for interactive and realistic representations of complex environments. I am also interested in exploring how simulation and immersive technologies can support applications such as spatial decision-making, urban and environmental modeling, digital twins, and interactive geospatial systems. Previously, I have also worked in Applied Cryptography and Information Security, which has given me a background in secure systems and data protection.

I am particularly interested in PhD research at the intersection of GIS, spatial simulation, GeoAI, and human-environment systems, with a focus on developing inclusive technologies for marginalised groups, including LGBTQ+ communities, and understanding how spatial, social, and environmental factors shape their experiences over time.
