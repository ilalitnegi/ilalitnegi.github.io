Research

My research sits at the intersection of three areas: LGBTQ+ rights and social justice, social XR, and GIS. I want to understand the history, lived experiences and changing needs of LGBTQ+ communities, and the social, legal, cultural and spatial factors that shape inclusion and inequality. Social XR (shared virtual, augmented and mixed reality spaces) interests me as a way to build safe, supportive places for connection, especially for people who may not have such spaces offline. GIS and spatial analysis let me study where safety, access and inclusion exist or are missing, and how this changes across places. I am also mindful of privacy and the responsible use of sensitive community data. I am open to shaping specific research questions around the needs and context of the PhD.

  • LGBTQ+ rights and social justice. The history, culture, rights and lived experiences of LGBTQ+ communities, and the social and legal factors that shape inclusion, safety and inequality.
  • Social XR. Shared virtual, augmented and mixed reality spaces for connection, support and community, with attention to presence, safety, accessibility and inclusive design.
  • GIS. Spatial analysis, web and 3D GIS, and mapping of inclusive spaces, safety and access across places and over time.

2026 Rainbow Connect Inclusive technology · Web application

A web-based communication platform designed to support LGBTQ+ communities in offices and institutions. The platform enables users to connect through text-based communication and video calling, with support for both Hindi and English. Designed as an inclusive digital space to encourage communication, connection, and peer interaction within institutional environments.

Inclusive Technology, Web Application, LGBTQ+, Chat, Video Calling, Hindi, English

2026 Indradhanush Spaces GIS · Inclusive technology

A web-based GIS application for discovering and registering LGBTQ+-inclusive spaces. Space owners can register their inclusive spaces on the platform, while users can explore nearby spaces through an interactive map. The application uses location-based visualization to display spaces around the user's current location and identify places within a selected geographic range.

GIS, Inclusive Technology, Web GIS, LGBTQ+, Location-Based Services, Interactive Mapping, Spatial Search
